01 / ABOUT

A scholar rooted in tradition, working toward contemporary questions.

I am a researcher and author based in Cairo, with an academic foundation in Islamic Theology and advanced study in Comparative Religion and Philosophy at Al-Azhar University.

My work explores how classical Islamic intellectual traditions can engage critically with contemporary philosophical, social, and political questions. I am particularly interested in comparative theology, Islamic philosophy, Ibn Khaldun’s social thought, Islamic political philosophy, and the relationship between faith and reason.

Alongside research and writing, I teach and participate in academic initiatives designed to create serious spaces for intellectual exchange.
